# Robotics, Control and Digital Twins

**Core question:** Does a simulated policy remain safe and effective under physical uncertainty?

**Claim boundary:** Simulator performance is not an airworthiness, industrial or classified-use approval.

## Evaluation axes

- Task success
- Safety-envelope violations
- Interventions
- Sim-to-real gap
- Fleet contention
